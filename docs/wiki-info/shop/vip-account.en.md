# VIP Account
For players who desire additional benefits beyond those offered by a premium account, there is another tier, VIP account.<br>
VIP account can be purchased exclusively with gold from the in-game virtual store by using the command [/shop].

!!! note "Note"
    Learn how you can buy gold by following the steps on [this page](buy-gold.md).

!!! warning "Warning"
    The prices in gold shown on this page are the standard ones. It is possible for items to be cheaper in-game if you purchase them during a sale period.



## Benefit Period and Prices

* 1 week will cost 200 Gold.
* 1 month will cost 750 Gold.
* 3 months will cost 2000 Gold.
* 6 months will cost 3750 Gold.
* 1 year will cost 7000 Gold.


!!! note "Note"
    Upon purchasing such a package, the features specific to a premium account will automatically become available to you.<br>
    You can also combine packages to extend the duration of the benefits—for example:<br>
    Purchasing the first two packages will cost you 950 Gold, and you will have access to premium account features for a period of one month and one week (37 days).



## VIP account benefits

The complete list of benefits offered by the VIP account can also be found in-game at any time via the command [*/vipaccount*].<br>
- The bank interest rate is increased to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">0.06</span>.<br>
- The bank interest thresholds increase to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">$15,000,000</span> or goods valued at least <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">$7,500,000</span>.<br>
- You will get a <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">50% bigger paycheck</span> every payday.<br>
- You will get <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">100% more money</span> from jobs and faction activities.<br>
- Every <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">2 hours played</span>, you receive: 1 Respect Point, 1 Rob Point, 1 Escape Point, 1 Accept Point and 1 less FP.<br>
- Extra Respect Points are kept for the next level when using <span style="color:#54FFCC;font-weight:bold;">/buylevel</span>.<br>
- You can buy/sell <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">Premium vehicles</span> from the Dealership or other players.<br>
- Your Rob Points limit increases to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">50</span>.<br>
- Your Escape Points limit increases to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">100</span>.<br>
- Your Accept Points limit increases to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">500</span>.<br>
- You can use <span style="color:#54FFCC;font-weight:bold;">/mp3</span> to listen to music outside vehicles.<br>
- You can fuel vehicles up to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">200%</span>.<br>
- You can start races using <span style="color:#54FFCC;font-weight:bold;">/race</span> with a stake up to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">$5,000</span>.<br>
- You can add up to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">200 friends</span> on <span style="color:#54FFCC;font-weight:bold;">/friends</span>.<br>
- You have access to the <span style="color:#54FFCC;font-weight:bold;">/vip</span> chat.<br>
- Your wanted level decreases every <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">13 minutes</span> instead of 15, before skin bonuses.<br>
- You can toggle a <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">colored name</span> in chat.<br>
- You can have up to <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">10 personal vehicles</span> spawned simultaneously.<br>
- You receive <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">80% of the vehicle's recorded price</span> when selling personal or clan vehicles back to the Dealership. Vehicles with a recorded price of $0 still sell for $0.<br>
- Your character profile on the website displays an <span style="color:#FFCF54;font-weight:bold;">animated skin</span>, performing an in-game animation.<br>


## VIP Chat Rules (/vip)
* The VIP chat [*/vip*] is visible to all VIP members on the server and is considered a global chat.
* To maintain a pleasant atmosphere among VIP members, stricter rules apply in addition to the general rules for global chats.
* We encourage all players with access to this chat to read the rules <a href="https://rules.b-zone.ro/en/#vip-chat">here</a>.
