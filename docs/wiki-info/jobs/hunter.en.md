# Hunter

## General Features
- Hunter is a new job added to the server, offering an open-world hunting experience.
- Players go through the following steps:
    - explore the hunting zones.
    - approach the freely roaming animals.
    - collect resources from the hunted animals.
    - choose between selling the resources and completing daily supply contracts.
- Hunting success depends on **precision** and **positioning**:
    - precise shots preserve carcass quality.
    - long-range shots provide extra resources and increase the chances of obtaining special resources.
- The job is built around **10 skill levels**. Higher levels increase:
    - weapon damage.
    - ammo capacity.
    - resource selling prices.
- The Hunter job has also been added to the **Daily Job** rotation.


## Skill Progression
- Skill 2 requires **30** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 3 requires **90** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 4 requires **210** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 5 requires **450** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 6 requires **650** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 7 requires **900** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 8 requires **1,200** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 9 requires **1,550** Job Skill Points.
- Skill 10 requires **1,900** Job Skill Points.
- These requirements represent the **total** Hunter Skill Points accumulated to reach each level.


### Progression Overview
- **Skill 1 – 5**: players can hunt and skin **cows**.
- **Skill 6**: hunting and skinning **deer** becomes available.
- **Skill 1 – 10**: **daily supply contracts** are available, with orders selected based on the player's skill.
- Higher levels provide:
    - increased weapon damage;
    - more ammo;
    - better selling prices.
- Progression rewards obtained from sales increase at **skill 5** and **skill 7**.


## Hunting Zones and Animal Behavior
### Hunting Zones
Active hunting zones are **marked on the player's map** when work begins.<br>
The system randomly selects **5–7 active** hunting zones.

### Animal Behavior
- Animals roam through the surroundings.
- Animals detect nearby threats and react to gunfire.
- Threatened animals can:
    - become alert.
    - flee.
    - make evasive movements.
- Panic can spread to nearby animals.
- Animals also have herd behavior:
    - they can follow each other.
    - they scatter when threatened.
    - they regroup afterwards.
- Their movement takes terrain and obstacles into account.

## Varieties and Rarity
Both cows and deer have different visual varieties and rarity levels.<br>
Animal characteristics and the amounts of resources obtained differ depending on the breed or variety.

## Hunting Equipment
### Weapon
- Hunters receive a **scoped rifle** when work begins.
- Rifle damage increases with the Hunter skill.
- The area where the animal is hit affects the damage:
    - **head** shots: increased damage.
    - **body** shots: normal damage.
    - **leg** shots: reduced damage.

### Ammo
- The ammo received at the start increases with skill:
    - Skill 1: **25** bullets
    - Skill 2: **30** bullets
    - Skill 3: **35** bullets
    - Skill 4: **40** bullets
    - Skill 5: **45** bullets
    - Skill 6: **50** bullets
    - Skill 7: **55** bullets
    - Skill 8: **60** bullets
    - Skill 9: **65** bullets
    - Skill 10: **70** bullets

### Resupply
- Players receive a warning when they have 1 bullet left.
- Return to the ammo supplier and press **Y** to resupply when you have exactly 1 bullet left.
- Using the last bullet ends the current Hunter work session.


## Carcass Quality and Skinning
### Carcass Quality Score
- Every killed animal receives a carcass quality score between **0 and 100**.
- The score depends on:
    - the number of shots.
    - the areas where the animal was hit.
- Precise **head** shots preserve quality.
- Repeated shots and **body** or **leg** shots reduce it.

### Quality Classification
- **Flawless**: 90–100
- **Excellent**: 75–89
- **Good**: 60–74
- **Damaged**: 40–59
- **Poor**: 20–39
- **Ruined**: below 20

### Quality Effects
Quality influences the amount of resources and the chance of obtaining special resources.<br>
Carcasses with a quality below 20 do not provide regular leather.

### Skinning
Approach a carcass and press **ALT** to skin it.<br>
Mobile players can use the command *[/skin]*.<br>
Skinning includes an animation and a progress indicator.

### Resources Obtained from Skinning
**Cows** provide: meat, leather, fat and bones.<br>
**Deer** provide: venison, leather, antlers and bones.<br>

### Carcass Availability
Carcasses remain available for **4–5 minutes**.<br>
During the first **2 minutes**, the carcass is **reserved** for the player who killed the animal.<br>
After that, other eligible Hunters can skin it.<br>
A carcass can be skinned by **only one player** at a time.

### Skinning Summary
- After skinning, players receive a summary with:
    - the resources collected.
    - the carcass quality.
    - the distance of the shot that killed the animal.
    - the shot statistics.


## Long-Range Hunting Bonuses
### Resource Amount Bonus
- The distance of the shot that kills the animal increases the amount of resources:
    - 25–49 meters: **+3%**
    - 50–74 meters: **+7%**
    - 75–99 meters: **+12%**
    - 100–149 meters: **+18%**
    - 150 meters or more: **+25%**

### Special Resource Chance Bonus
- Killing animals from a distance also increases the chance of obtaining eligible special resources:
    - 50–74 meters: **+1 percentage point**
    - 75–99 meters: **+2 percentage points**
    - 100–149 meters: **+4 percentage points**
    - 150 meters or more: **+6 percentage points**

!!! note "Note"
    These bonuses reward precise long-range shots.<br>
    Carcass quality still affects the resources obtained.


## Hunting Merchant and Payments
### Selling Resources
- Players can sell the collected resources to the **Hunting Merchant** by pressing **Y**.
- The interface shows the owned amounts and the current prices per unit.
- Selling options:
    - selling a chosen amount.
    - Sell all.
- Both options require **confirmation** before the sale is finalized.

### Base Prices per Unit
- The following values are the base prices per unit, before the selling bonuses provided by the Hunter skill and the other money bonuses of the job.
- Common resources:
    - Cow meat: **$16**
    - Venison: **$21**
    - Leather: **$28**
    - Fat: **$9**
    - Bones: **$7**
    - Antlers: **$50**
    - Milk: **$7**
- Special resources:
    - Quality Leather: **$90**
    - Rare Leather: **$180**
    - Exotic Leather: **$330**
    - Trophy Horn: **$450**
    - Fine Deer Leather: **$100**
    - Golden Horn: **$280**
    - Albino Fur: **$500**
    - Spirit Horn: **$675**

### Selling Bonus by Skill
- The Hunter skill increases the selling price per unit:
    - Skill 1: base price
    - Skill 2: **+3%**
    - Skill 3: **+6%**
    - Skill 4: **+10%**
    - Skill 5: **+13%**
    - Skill 6: **+16%**
    - Skill 7: **+20%**
    - Skill 8: **+23%**
    - Skill 9: **+26%**
    - Skill 10: **+30%**

### Payment Calculation
Prices per unit are calculated in **whole dollars**.<br>
Fractional values are **rounded down**.<br>
The skill-adjusted price per unit is multiplied by the number of resources sold to calculate the **base payment**.<br>
The money bonuses applicable to the job are added **afterwards**, to calculate the total payment.

### Statistics and Goals
Sales are recorded in the job statistics.<br>
Sales contribute to the **Job Goal** and to the progress of applicable tasks.


## Hunter Rewards and Progression
### How Reward Cycles Work
- Selling hunting resources accumulates progress based on their **standard base value**, before the selling and money bonuses.
- Every **$1,500** of accumulated base value completes a **reward cycle**.
- Progress is kept between sales, so players can sell smaller amounts without losing the accumulated progress.
- A larger sale can complete **multiple cycles at once**.
- Both individual sales and the **Sell all** option follow the same rules.

### Rewards for Each $1,500 Cycle
- Skill 1: 1 Skill Point / 1 Clan XP / 1 Marathon Point
- Skill 2: 1 Skill Point / 1 Clan XP / 1 Marathon Point
- Skill 3: 1 Skill Point / 1 Clan XP / 1 Marathon Point
- Skill 4: 1 Skill Point / 1 Clan XP / 1 Marathon Point
- Skill 5: 2 Skill Points / 2 Clan XP / 2 Marathon Points
- Skill 6: 2 Skill Points / 2 Clan XP / 2 Marathon Points
- Skill 7: 3 Skill Points / 4 Clan XP / 4 Marathon Points
- Skill 8: 3 Skill Points / 4 Clan XP / 4 Marathon Points
- Skill 9: 3 Skill Points / 4 Clan XP / 4 Marathon Points
- Skill 10: 3 Skill Points / 4 Clan XP / 4 Marathon Points

### Important Notes
- The listed skill rewards are **before the skin bonuses are applied**.
- Every completed cycle counts as a **successful run for Job Clash**.
- Money bonuses increase the payment, but they do **not** increase the base value used for progression.

### Example
- A **skill 7** Hunter sells resources with a base value of **$3,400**.
- They complete **two cycles** and receive:
    - 6 Skill Points.
    - 8 Clan XP.
    - 8 Marathon Points.
- The remaining **$400** is kept for the next cycle.

### Milk Exception
Milk does **not** contribute to the Hunter progression cycles.<br>
This rule also applies when milk is sold through the **Sell all** option.


## Daily Supply Contracts
### General Rules
- Hunters can access the daily supply contracts starting from **skill 1**.
- Each player receives a random order suited to their Hunter skill.
- Higher skill levels unlock larger orders and requests that include resources from deer.
- The contract interface shows:
    - the required amounts.
    - the resources owned.
    - the delivery status.
- Players must collect **all the required materials** before completing the contract.
- Resources sold to the Merchant are **no longer available** for delivery.
- Each player can complete **one contract per day**.

### Reward Packages
- Before delivery, players choose one of **eight reward packages**:
    - **Premium Payment** - 220% of the base market value of the required materials.
    - **Hunter Training**
        - 110% of the base market value;
        - plus 15–30 Hunter Skill Points.
    - **Respect Package**
        - 125% of the base market value;
        - plus Respect Points equal to the Hunter skill.
    - **Mission Package**
        - 125% of the base market value;
        - plus 10 + the Hunter skill Mission Points.
    - **Wood for Craftsman**
        - 100% of the base market value;
        - plus 20 + 5 × the Hunter skill Wood Materials.
    - **Cotton for Craftsman**
        - 100% of the base market value;
        - plus 20 + 5 × the Hunter skill Cotton Materials.
    - **Silver for Craftsman**
        - 100% of the base market value;
        - plus 30 + 6 × the Hunter skill Silver Materials.
    - **Gold for Craftsman**
        - 100% of the base market value;
        - plus 20 + 5 × the Hunter skill Gold Materials.

### Important Notes
- Craftsman rewards respect the applicable material storage limits.
- Gold for Craftsman represents crafting materials, not the premium Gold currency.
- Contract payments use the standard resource prices and are separate from the regular selling bonuses at the Merchant.
- Contract deliveries do not trigger the regular sales progression cycles.
- The Hunter Training package provides its own skill reward.
    - An order requiring resources worth **$10,000** at base prices gives **$22,000** if the **Premium Payment** package is chosen.

## Milking
### Conditions
**Rare**, **Epic** and **Legendary** cows can be milked when they are **calm** and **eligible**.<br>
A cow cannot be milked again until the milk availability resets.

### How to Milk
Use the **crouch key** next to the cow.<br>
Mobile players use the **/milk** command.<br>
Milking takes approximately **4.5 seconds**.

### Milk Amount by Rarity
Rare Cows: **1–2** units.<br>
Epic Cows: **2–3** units.<br>
Legendary Cows: **3–5** units.

### Rewards
Each collected unit of milk gives **10 Farmer Skill Points**.

### Selling Milk
- Milk can be sold to the Merchant.
- Milk benefits from the applicable selling and money bonuses.
- Selling milk does **not** provide:
    - Hunter progression cycles;
    - Hunter Skill Points;
    - Marathon Points;
    - Clan XP from these cycles.

## Riding Animals
Riding an animal triggers an animal-specific audio effect.<br>
Playtime accumulation is paused while you ride an animal.<br>
Playtime accumulation resumes after dismounting.


## Specific Commands
### /skin
Alternative to the **ALT** key for mobile players.<br>
It is used for skinning carcasses.

### /milk
Alternative to the **crouch** key for mobile players.<br>
It is used for milking cows.


!!! note "Note"
    Both commands follow the same eligibility checks and interaction rules as their keyboard counterparts.
